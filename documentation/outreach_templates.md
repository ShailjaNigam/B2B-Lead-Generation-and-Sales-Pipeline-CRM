# Outreach templates and follow-up strategy

These templates are built into the CRM's **Outreach** page, which fills in the contact, company and rep name automatically.
Use them only for business contacts who can reasonably expect to hear from you. Respect opt-outs and email and privacy rules.

## 1. First outreach email
**Subject:** Simplifying HR operations at [Company Name]

Hello [Contact Name],

I hope you are doing well.

I am reaching out to introduce our HR Management Software, designed to help businesses streamline employee management, attendance tracking, payroll processing, and HR operations.

We believe our solution could help [Company Name] improve operational efficiency and reduce manual HR processes.

Would you be available for a short 15-minute discussion to explore whether our solution could benefit your organization?

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Rep Name], Sales Development Team

## 2. Follow-up email
**Subject:** Following up: HR software for [Company Name]

Hello [Contact Name],

I wanted to follow up on my previous email regarding our HR Management Software.

I would be happy to demonstrate how our solution can simplify HR operations and improve workforce management.

Please let me know if you would be available for a brief discussion.

Best regards,
[Rep Name], Sales Development Team

## 3. Meeting confirmation
**Subject:** Confirmed: PeopleFirst HR product demo

Hello [Contact Name],

Thank you for your interest in our HR Management Software.

This email confirms our scheduled product demonstration. During the session, we will cover:
- Employee management features
- Attendance and payroll automation
- Reporting capabilities
- Pricing and implementation

Looking forward to speaking with you.

Best regards,
[Rep Name], Sales Development Team

## Follow-up cadence and what the data shows

| Day | Activity | Reply rate in the sample data |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Initial outreach | 12.9% |
| Day 3 | First follow-up | 14.1% |
| Day 7 | Second follow-up | 12.6% |
| Day 10 | Call to schedule a product demo | 8.6% |
| Day 14 | Final follow-up, then update lead status | 0.0% |

**Takeaway:** most replies arrive in the first week. After Day 14, mark the lead *Lost: No response* so the pipeline stays accurate. You can revisit it next quarter.
